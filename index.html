      The actual point of this website

      A letter

      Anishka,

      We met over Theory of Computation. A shared study group, a subject most of our batch was simply trying to get past, and somehow the most important thing that has ever happened to me came out of it. I have never once been able to say that out loud without it sounding absurd. It is still true.

      Every year I try to find something to give you that feels like it's actually worth your time, and every year I fall short — because there isn't a thing on this planet that scales to how much you matter to me. So I built you a place instead.

      You are the person who makes ordinary days feel like they're worth photographing. You laugh at your own jokes before you finish them. You notice when I'm off before I do. You fight for the people you love, quietly, without ever asking for credit. That is the whole personality of someone genuinely good, and I get a front-row seat to it.

      I hope twenty-six is kind to you. I hope it gives you the things you've been too polite to ask for. And I hope you never, not once, forget how loved you are — by me, and by everyone lucky enough to be in your orbit.

      Happy birthday, my love. Here's to another year of you.

      Always yours,
      Prasuk
